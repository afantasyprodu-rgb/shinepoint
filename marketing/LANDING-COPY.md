## Section — The math everywhere else

> ### Everywhere else, you pay before you know there's a job.
>
> Lead-gen platforms charge you $10 to $200 to be handed a phone number. If the customer
> ghosts — and most do — that's your money, gone. Share the same job with four other pros
> and every one of them gets charged the moment the customer taps a button.
>
> You're bidding with your own cash on a job you might not get.
>
> **ShinePoint charges you nothing to receive a booking.** A customer books, you accept,
> you do the work, you get paid. Our 15% comes out of a completed job — so if you didn't
> earn, we didn't either.

## Section — Getting paid

> ### Your money moves on a schedule you can plan around.
>
> **Your cut clears in 48 hours.** After that it sits in your balance until you want it.
>
> **Withdraw any amount, any time, from inside the app.** Not a redirect to somebody
> else's dashboard. Not net-30. Not a Friday-only batch. You type a number, you hit
> withdraw, it goes to your bank.
>
> **Tips are yours and we don't touch them.** 100%, paid instantly, no fee to receive them.
>
> **You never send an invoice to chase a payment.** The customer pays in the app before
> you start driving.

## Section — It runs the business, not just the calendar

Four cards:

**Itemized invoices**
Build a line-item invoice on the job, save it as a template, reuse it next time. The
customer sees exactly what they paid for — and so do you at tax time.

**Your own promo codes**
Issue discounts on your terms, when you want the work. Slow Tuesday? Your call, not ours.

**A loyalty program that's yours**
Repeat customers earn rewards and come back to *you*. You're building a book of business,
not renting one.

**Earnings analytics**
Take-home per job, week over week. Know what you actually make, not what you grossed.

## Detailer FAQ

**What does ShinePoint cost me?**
15% of a completed booking. There is no signup fee, no monthly subscription, no lead fee,
and no charge for receiving or declining a job.

**When do I actually get my money?**
Your cut clears 48 hours after the job completes. Then you withdraw whatever amount you
want, whenever you want, from inside the app.

**Do you take part of my tip?**
No. 100% of every tip goes to you, instantly, and we don't charge a fee to receive it.

**Do I have to use the app, or can I use my phone browser?**
The native app, for detailers. Live location tracking has to keep running while your
screen is locked on the drive to the job, and a browser can't do that reliably.

**Do I set my own prices?**
Yes. Detailers set their prices and their own service menu.

**Can I keep my existing customers?**
Yes — and the loyalty tools are built to help you keep them coming back to you specifically.
