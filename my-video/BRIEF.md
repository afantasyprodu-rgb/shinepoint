## Intent

Motion graphics promo showcasing ShinePoint's client-side security — specifically the real-time identity verification that ensures every detailer on the platform is verified. The video speaks directly to the customer's core anxiety: "Is it safe to let a stranger come to my home?"

### Key beats

1. **Hook (0-5s):** Open with the anxiety — "You're about to let a stranger come to your home. Do you trust them?"
2. **Problem (5-10s):** Other platforms let anyone sign up. No real checks. No accountability.
3. **Solution (10-25s):** ShinePoint's verification flow — every detailer uploads their ID, we verify it matches them in real-time. Verified badge = proven identity.
4. **Trust payoff (25-35s):** "Every detailer is verified. Your home stays safe." Show the verified badge as a trust anchor.
5. **CTA (35-45s):** ShinePoint — Book with confidence. Download now.

### Tone & Style

- Clean, confident, premium — not flashy, not corporate
- Brand colors: Primary purple (#7C3AED), accent green (#22C55E), background light purple (#FAF5FF)
- Typography: Lexend (headings), Source Sans 3 (body)
- Motion: smooth, deliberate, no frantic energy
- Visual language: shields, verification icons, checkmarks, trust signals

### Do NOT mention

- Stripe as the verification provider (we say "our verification system" or "real-time ID verification")
- Any technical backend details

- Primary: 16:9 (YouTube, website embed)
- Secondary: 9:16 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)
